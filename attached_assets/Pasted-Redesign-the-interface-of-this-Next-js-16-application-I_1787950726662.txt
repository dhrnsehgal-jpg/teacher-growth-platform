## The actual goal

This is a professional growth platform for schoolteachers. The same data can
read as "we are helping you develop" or "we are scoring you", and the difference
is entirely design. The teacher looking at it is seeing their own professional
record, which their manager can also see, and which feeds their appraisal.

Make it feel like support without hiding anything or softening a real gap.
Warm and honest at the same time. That is the brief. "Minimal and neat" is the
surface of it.

## Rules specific to this product — not general good practice

- A gap never appears without its route out next to it. Same screen.
- Never use red or alarm colours for a person's competency level. A teacher
  below expectation is not an error state.
- Show verified level and expected level as two points on a journey, never as
  pass/fail.
- Verified growth should feel like an event. An open gap should feel like a task.
- Analytics must NOT rank people. No sorted bar charts of named staff, no
  leaderboards. Cohorts and distributions only. This is a product rule.
- Where an expectation comes from (NPST, CBSE, school-defined) always travels
  with it.
- "Requires verification" must be as visible as "verified", never muted.

## Do not break these

1. These strings must appear VERBATIM — legal and ethical commitments, not copy.
   Restyle them, give them a proper callout component — but do not shorten,
   paraphrase, hide behind a disclosure, or make them less prominent than the
   number they qualify:

   "DEMO SCHOOL POLICY — NOT A CBSE OR PUNJAB GOVERNMENT FORMULA."
   "AI-assisted recommendation — professional judgement required."
   "Employment/service-rule applicability requires authorised verification."
   "School funding/service status requires verification before employment-related
    compliance calculations can be activated."

2. Never let colour alone carry meaning. Verification status, plan stages,
   milestones, appraisal steps, form success/failure and heatmap cells must all
   remain readable without colour. Each was a real defect, found and fixed.

3. Accessibility must not regress. Text 4.5:1, control borders and focus
   indicators 3:1, light and dark. tests/unit/contrast.test.ts recomputes every
   pair from globals.css and will fail you. Keep the skip link, aria-current,
   per-page titles, 320px reflow, keyboard-reachable scroll regions.

4. Do not touch src/lib/data/, src/lib/supabase/, src/app/actions/,
   src/middleware.ts, or supabase/migrations/. Presentation only.

5. No component library needing a build change, no CSS-in-JS runtime.
   Tailwind v3 + hand-rolled components.
