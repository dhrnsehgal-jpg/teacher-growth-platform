# Design Brief

A prompt for redesigning the interface, and the reasoning behind its
constraints. Paste the block in [Prompt](#prompt) into the Replit Agent.

---

## The mandate, before the aesthetics

The product brief this platform was built to ends with a sentence that is a
design instruction more than a feature one:

> The final MVP should function as **a professional growth and school
> improvement system**, not merely **a teacher scoring system**.

The same data reads as either, depending entirely on hierarchy, tone, colour and
adjacency. A teacher opening a page that says they are below expectation on five
competencies is in a vulnerable position — this is their professional record,
their appraisal feeds it, and their head of department can see it. Whether that
page feels like support or like surveillance is decided by design, and almost
nothing else.

So "neat, minimal, organised" is the surface. The actual brief is: **make a
system that could be used to judge people feel like one that exists to develop
them** — without hiding anything, softening a real gap, or making the numbers
harder to find. Honesty and warmth at the same time. That is the hard part and
it is worth saying out loud, because a generic visual polish pass will not do it
and may well work against it.

---

## The four consumers, from their end

### The teacher — the primary user

**Context.** A phone, or a shared staffroom computer. Four minutes between
classes. Possibly anxious about what it says.

**The moment.** They open it because someone told them to, or because appraisal
is coming. The first screen decides whether they ever come back voluntarily.

**Seamless means:** they get their answer without hunting, and every answer that
names a problem also names the next step. Nobody should have to interpret a
number to find out whether they are all right.

### The head of department — the highest-volume user

**Context.** A laptop, twenty minutes on a free period, working through a queue:
approvals, evidence to verify, observations to record.

**The moment.** They have six things to action and want to leave with zero.

**Seamless means:** the queue is obvious, each item is actionable without
leaving the page, and finishing one advances the list. Batch work should feel
like batch work.

### The principal — an occasional, scanning user

**Context.** Looking for patterns across 23 staff, before a meeting.

**The moment.** "Where does this school need help?"

**Seamless means:** the shape of the school is legible in seconds, and
individuals are reachable but never the headline. **Analytics must not look like
a league table** — that is a product rule, not a preference.

### The compliance administrator — a precision user

**Context.** Working through the regulatory register or the audit trail, where
being approximately right is worthless.

**Seamless means:** status is unambiguous, provenance is always visible, and
verified is never visually confusable with unverified.

## What must survive, and why

This is not a greenfield redesign. The application is complete, tested, and
several of its visual elements carry legal or ethical weight rather than
decorative weight.

### Strings that must appear, verbatim

Each is a commitment the platform makes. Rewording them changes what the school
is claiming.

| String                                                                                                                    | Where                               | Why                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `DEMO SCHOOL POLICY — NOT A CBSE OR PUNJAB GOVERNMENT FORMULA.`                                                           | Everywhere the growth score appears | Distinguishes a school's own model from national policy |
| `AI-assisted recommendation — professional judgement required.`                                                           | Every assistant output              | Required of all AI output influencing development       |
| `Employment/service-rule applicability requires authorised verification.`                                                 | Increment and service pages         | The employment gate                                     |
| `School funding/service status requires verification before employment-related compliance calculations can be activated.` | Increment pages                     | The funding gate                                        |

They may be restyled — better typography, better placement, a proper callout
component. They may not be shortened, paraphrased, moved behind a disclosure, or
made less prominent than the figure they qualify.

### Meaning that must not move into colour alone

Every one of these was an accessibility defect that was found and fixed:

- Verification status (verified / requires verification / superseded)
- Plan-item stage, learning milestones, appraisal steps
- Whether a form action succeeded or was refused
- Heatmap cells

Colour may reinforce. It must never be the only carrier. There are tests.

### Guarantees currently under test

Ten accessibility specs run axe against 21 pages across five roles, plus
keyboard bypass, `aria-current`, distinct page titles, 320px reflow and WCAG
1.4.12 text spacing. Eight colour pairs are locked by a unit test that
recomputes contrast from the stylesheet.

**A redesign that lowers any of these fails.** The palette must clear 4.5:1 for
text and 3:1 for control borders and focus indicators, in light and dark, and
the test will say so.

## Design rules specific to this product

These follow from the mandate and are not general good practice.

1. **A gap never appears without its route out adjacent to it.** Not on another
   screen, not behind a tab.
2. **Never use red, or any alarm colour, for a person's own competency level.**
   Reserve alarm styling for system and compliance states. A teacher two levels
   below expectation is not an error condition.
3. **Show the journey, not the verdict.** Verified level and expected level read
   as two points on a path, never as pass/fail.
4. **Growth gets weight; deficit gets calm.** A verified reassessment should
   feel like an event. An open gap should feel like a task.
5. **Analytics must not rank people.** No sorted bar charts of named staff, no
   leaderboards, no podium metaphors. Cohorts and distributions only.
6. **Provenance is never decoration.** Where an expectation comes from — NPST,
   CBSE, school-defined — travels with it.
7. **Uncertainty is shown, not smoothed.** "Requires verification" must be as
   visible as "verified", never a muted afterthought.

---

## Sequence

Foundations before screens. A redesign that starts with the dashboard produces
27 inconsistent pages.

1. **Tokens** — type scale, spacing, radius, elevation, and a palette that meets
   the contrast tests. Extend `globals.css` and `tailwind.config.ts`; there are
   19 tokens today, which is too few to hold a system together.
2. **Primitives** — Card, Button, Badge, Field, Table, EmptyState, StatusPill,
   Callout. Seven hand-rolled components exist (`src/components/`); grow them
   into a real set. `clsx`, `tailwind-merge` and `lucide-react` are installed;
   `recharts` is installed and unused.
3. **The shell** — navigation is currently twenty flat links for every role.
   This is the single biggest structural problem. Group by task, and show people
   only what their role can reach.
4. **The teacher dashboard**, then the Learning Map, then a competency detail
   page. Three screens, reviewed together, before going wider.
5. **Everything else**, in role order: manager queue → leadership analytics →
   admin and compliance.

## Do not break these

1. These strings must appear VERBATIM. They are legal and ethical commitments,
   not copy. Restyle them, make them prettier, give them a proper callout
   component — but do not shorten, paraphrase, hide behind a disclosure, or make
   them less prominent than the number they qualify:

   "DEMO SCHOOL POLICY — NOT A CBSE OR PUNJAB GOVERNMENT FORMULA."
   "AI-assisted recommendation — professional judgement required."
   "Employment/service-rule applicability requires authorised verification."
   "School funding/service status requires verification before employment-related
    compliance calculations can be activated."

2. Never let colour alone carry meaning. Verification status, plan stages,
   milestones, appraisal steps, form success/failure and heatmap cells must all
   remain readable without colour. Each of these was a real defect that was
   found and fixed. There are tests.

3. Accessibility must not regress. Text 4.5:1, control borders and focus
   indicators 3:1, light and dark. tests/unit/contrast.test.ts recomputes every
   pair from globals.css and will fail you. Keep the skip link, aria-current,
   per-page titles, 320px reflow, and keyboard-reachable scroll regions.

4. Do not touch src/lib/data/, src/lib/supabase/, src/app/actions/,
   src/middleware.ts, or supabase/migrations/. This is presentation only.

5. Do not add a component library that needs a build step change, and do not
   introduce a CSS-in-JS runtime. Tailwind v3 + hand-rolled components.
