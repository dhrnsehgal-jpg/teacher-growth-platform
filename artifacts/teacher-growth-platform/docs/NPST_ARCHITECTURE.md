## 1. What is established, and what is not

**Established:**

- NEP 2020 tasks NCTE, in its restructured form as a Professional Standard Setting
  Body, with developing National Professional Standards for Teachers.
- NCTE publishes an _NPST Guiding Document, 2023_ at
  `https://ncte.gov.in/website/PDF/NPST/NPST-Book.pdf`, alongside earlier 2021
  material.
- NCERT's verified 2022 CPD guidelines state that a teacher's CPD e-portfolio "will
  be linked to their Career Management and Progression (CMP) and National
  Professional Standards for Teachers (NPST)" — confirming NPST is intended to
  connect CPD, appraisal and career progression, which is precisely this product's
  lifecycle.

**Not established:**

- The NPST domains, standards, career stages and competency descriptors. **`ncte.gov.in`
  refused connections throughout Stage 1 research**, so the Guiding Document was not
  read.
- Whether NPST has been **notified as binding** on anyone, and specifically on
  private CBSE-affiliated schools.
- Whether the document is titled "guiding" because it is advisory, or because it
  guides implementation of something mandatory.

## 2. The rule this creates

> **An NPST recommendation must never be presented as mandatory CBSE law.**

Two distinct errors are possible here, and the architecture blocks both:

1. Treating NPST as binding when it is guidance. Blocked by classification: NPST
   requirements are recorded as `recommended` unless and until an authoritative
   source establishes otherwise.
2. Treating NPST as _CBSE's_. NPST is an **NCTE** instrument at the **central**
   layer. Recording it under the CBSE authority would misattribute it. The seeded
   source sits under the `ncte` authority with `layer = 'central'`.

## 3. Design position: mapping, not adoption

The school's competency framework is **the school's own**. NPST enters as a
_mapping_, never as the framework itself.

```
competency_framework            (the school's, versioned)
   └── competency_domain
        └── competency
             └── competency_indicator
                        │
                        │  competency_standard_mapping
                        ▼
             external standard reference
             (framework = 'NPST', version, domain/standard code,
              verification_status, mapping_confidence)
```

Why this way round:

- The school must be able to define what good teaching looks like in its own
  context — Balvatika through Class XII, Punjab, CBSE — without waiting on a
  national framework whose status is unclear.
- If NPST is later notified as binding, the mapping becomes the compliance bridge:
  the school demonstrates coverage rather than rebuilding its framework.
- If NPST changes, a mapping is cheap to re-version. A framework is not.
- A mapping row can carry its own `verification_status`. An unverified mapping is
  displayed as "indicative mapping, not verified" rather than silently implying
  alignment.

## 4. What the platform will show a teacher

While NPST is unverified, a competency screen shows:

- the school competency, its indicators and the target proficiency level — the
  school's own expectation, attributed to the school;
- **no** NPST claim of any kind.

Once NPST is verified and mapped, the same screen adds:

> Mapped to NPST [domain / standard reference] — _Recommended by NCTE (Central /
> National)_. This mapping is indicative and does not change the school's
> requirement.

The attribution line is generated by `describeRequirement()`, which cannot produce
"Required by" for a `recommended` classification. A test asserts that for every
authority layer.

## 5. Career stages

NPST is understood to define expectations at different career stages. The platform
already has `core.career_level` — an ordered, school-defined ladder seeded as
Entrant → Developing → Proficient → Expert → Lead Practitioner.

**These names are the school's, chosen as a reasonable starting point, and are not
claimed to be NPST's.** If NPST's stages turn out to differ, the school's ladder can
either be renamed or mapped; `career_level` is a table precisely so that this is a
data change rather than a migration.

## 7. Stage plan

- **Stage 2** — build the school's competency framework and the
  `competency_standard_mapping` structure. Leave NPST mappings empty.
- **After verification** — load NPST domains and standards as a
  `regulatory.source` plus `regulatory.requirement` rows classified according to
  what verification establishes, and populate mappings.
- **Stage 5** — if, and only if, NPST career stages are verified, align or map the
  school's career ladder to them.

Until then, the product answers "what is expected of me?" entirely from the school's
own framework — which is the correct answer regardless of NPST's status.

## 8. Addendum — what verification established

### Stage 2: the NPST Guiding Document was retrieved

Stage 1 could not reach `ncte.gov.in`. Stage 2 retrieved the document from NCTE's
CloudFront mirror and recorded it as a verified source (migration `0009`), which
resolved **verification actions 1 and 2**.

What it establishes, and the limit of it:

- NPST calls itself a **guiding document**. It is not notified law.
- It reaches this school only if Punjab designates an implementing entity —
  which is unverified, and is why NPST requirements are classified `recommended`
  with per-school applicability left undetermined.
- **Domains 9, 10 and 11 could not be extracted** from the PDF. They are recorded
  as missing rather than invented, so the reference framework is knowingly
  incomplete. This remains open.

Fifteen of the school's competencies are NPST-aligned and quote NPST's own
indicator text verbatim, cited by NPST number. Full position in
[`STAGE_2_COMPLETION.md`](STAGE_2_COMPLETION.md) §1 and
[`COMPETENCY_FRAMEWORK.md`](COMPETENCY_FRAMEWORK.md).

### Stage 3 addendum: CBSE does reference NPST — verification action 4

**Action 4 asked whether CBSE has issued anything adopting or referencing NPST.
It has.**

The verified CBSE CPD Guidelines 2025 (Notification TRG-02/2025) allocate the
50 annual CPD hours across three domains named:

| CBSE CPD domain                     | NPST Standard                       |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Core Values and Ethics              | Core Values and Ethics              |
| Knowledge and Practice              | Knowledge and Practice              |
| Professional Growth and Development | Professional Growth and Development |

These match NPST's three Standards by name. Recorded as requirement
`cbse.cpd.npst_alignment`.

### What this narrows, and what it does not

**Narrows:** the Stage 2 open question of whether NPST's structure has any
standing for a private CBSE school. It does — CBSE has adopted NPST's domain
names as the organising structure for mandatory CPD hours. The school's framework
spine, chosen in Stage 2 on the judgement that NPST's three Standards were a
reasonable structure, turns out to align with the structure CBSE actually uses.
That was a fortunate call rather than an informed one, and is worth recording as
such.

**Does not narrow:** NPST's own legal status. CBSE adopting NPST's _domain names
for allocating CPD hours_ is not CBSE mandating _NPST as an appraisal standard_,
and it is not Punjab designating an implementing entity. The distinction is
load-bearing for Stage 5: nothing here authorises tying an increment to an NPST
appraisal.

NPST requirement rows therefore stay `recommended`. Only the CPD-domain alignment
is `mandatory`, and only because CBSE says so in its own notification — and even
that is unenforced for this school pending affiliation verification.
