# Teacher's Guide

This platform exists to help you get better at teaching and to make your
development visible. It is not a scoring system, and it is not designed to
catch you out.

Two things are worth knowing before anything else:

- **A gap is never shown without a route out of it.** Every gap on your
  dashboard links to development that addresses it.
- **No number decides anything about your career.** Progression, appraisal
  outcomes and increments are judgements made by people. The platform shows the
  evidence those people use, and records what they decided.

---

## Signing in

Use the account your school gave you. Accounts cannot be created by signing up —
if you do not have one, ask the school office.

## Your dashboard

The top of your dashboard answers eight questions in plain sentences. Each one
links to the detail behind it.

| Question                           | What it tells you                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Where am I?                        | How many of your assessed competencies are at or above what is expected of your post |
| Where should I be?                 | The level expected for your role and stage                                           |
| What is my gap?                    | How many gaps are open, and which is largest                                         |
| What should I do next?             | The next step, and whether it is yours or your reviewer's                            |
| What CPD do I need?                | The activity that best matches your gaps                                             |
| Am I compliant?                    | Your CPD hours against the requirement                                               |
| Have I improved?                   | Verified reassessments so far                                                        |
| What is my next professional step? | Your career level, and how progression actually works                                |

Alongside them is a ring showing your CPD hours for the year.

Below that: your development priorities, your Learning Map, KPIs, growth trend,
recommended CPD, goals, evidence and recent feedback.

## Understanding a gap

A gap is the difference between the level **expected** for your post and the
level **verified** for you. It is arithmetic, not a judgement — the calculation
is deterministic and the same inputs always give the same result.

Open **Why is this a priority?** on any gap and you will see every factor that
contributed and how many points each added. Nothing is hidden, and no AI decides
the ranking.

Your verified level is not a single opinion. Open a competency and **How your
verified level was reached** lists every input separately — your
self-assessment, your supervisor's assessment, classroom observation — and who
verified the final level. None of them is silently merged into the others.

**Nobody verifies their own competency.** Your self-assessment is an input, and
an important one, but it is not the verified level.

## Self-assessment

Rate yourself honestly. An inflated self-assessment does not raise your verified
level — it just makes the conversation with your reviewer less useful.

Previous self-assessments are never overwritten. The history is part of your
professional record and shows how your own view of your practice has developed.

## Your Learning Map

This is your development plan for the year. Each item moves through stages:

```
Proposed → Approved → Learning → Completed → Reflected → Applied
        → Impact verified → Reassessed
```

The order matters, and one step in it is the point of the whole design:

> **Completing a course does not improve your competency level.**

There is no path from _Completed_ straight to _Reassessed_. You attend, you
reflect, you apply it in your teaching, somebody verifies that it reached your
practice — and only then is the competency reassessed. This is not an obstacle
course. It is the difference between having attended training and having become
better at something.

### Adding an activity

Open a development priority from your dashboard, look at **Recommended
development**, and click **Add to my Learning Map**. Each recommendation shows
**Why this course?** with its reasons.

Your reviewer approves it before it starts.

### Reflection and application

After completing an activity you record two things:

- **Reflection** — what landed, and what you intend to change.
- **Application** — what you actually changed in your teaching.

Write these for yourself, not for the record. A reflection that says what
genuinely shifted is worth more at your appraisal than one written to sound
right.

## Evidence

Evidence is what shows your practice changed. A revised assessment, a lesson
sequence, student work, an observation.

Submit it against the plan item it relates to. Your reviewer verifies it,
returns it for clarification, or explains why it does not evidence what it was
submitted for. If it comes back, the reason is on the record with it.

## CPD

Your CPD page shows your hours against the requirement, split by where the
training came from and which domain it belongs to.

**Hours do not count until they are verified.** Logging an activity records it;
your head of department or coordinator confirms it, and only then does it
credit.

A shortfall in one part is not offset by a surplus in another. Twenty-five extra
school-organised hours do not cover a shortfall in board-provided hours, because
the requirement specifies both.

> The CBSE CPD requirements are recorded and verified, but they are **not
> currently enforced** for this school — the school's CBSE affiliation status is
> unverified. Your hours are tracked; no compliance consequence follows from
> them yet.

## Your appraisal

Your appraisal page shows the outcome and the components behind it, with the
weight and result of each.

The growth score carries this label wherever it appears:

> **DEMO SCHOOL POLICY — NOT A CBSE OR PUNJAB GOVERNMENT FORMULA.**

That is exactly what it is. It is your school's own model, not a rule from CBSE
or the Punjab Government, and it should be argued with if you think it is wrong.

### If you disagree

You can **acknowledge** the appraisal, or you can **challenge** it. If you
challenge it, your representation is reviewed independently by the Principal.

**Your original appraisal is never deleted, and neither is your response.** An
earlier response stays on the file when you add another. The record is the whole
conversation, not its last state.

## Your service record and career progression

Your service record shows your career history and which rules the school has
determined apply to you.

You will see that several items say **Requires Verification**. That is honest,
not broken: the school's funding status and the applicability of Punjab service
rules have not been established, so the platform declines to apply them. It will
not guess about your employment terms.

**Career progression is not calculated.** It is discussed at appraisal. The
platform shows what has been recorded; it does not compute who gets promoted.

## The Growth Assistant

The assistant can explain your gaps and feedback, suggest goals, help you draft
a plan, summarise your reflections and evidence, and explain your CPD position.

Every suggestion it makes is labelled:

> **AI-assisted recommendation — professional judgement required.**

It shows you which of your records it used. If it cannot explain something from
your actual records, it says so rather than inventing an explanation.

**What it cannot do**, structurally — not by policy, but because the system
provides no mechanism:

- Change any competency score
- Create a hidden score of any kind
- Make an appraisal decision, promote you, or affect your pay
- Override a decision a person made
- State a CBSE or Punjab requirement that is not verified in the register

**Your data does not leave the school's system.** External AI assistance is off
by default and cannot be enabled without a named person recording a provider,
a data region, a processing agreement and a privacy review. The assistant page
says where your data goes.

## Who has seen your record

Your profile page has a panel showing **who has opened your record** — when
somebody else opened your pay or appraisal file, and why.

Opening your own record is not logged. This list is always other people.

## Accessibility

The platform targets WCAG 2.2 AA. It works with a keyboard alone: the first Tab
on any page is **Skip to main content**. It works at 320px and when you enlarge
text or force your own line spacing. Tables scroll with the keyboard, not only
with a mouse.

If something is unusable for you, tell the school administrator. See
`ACCESSIBILITY.md` for what has been tested and what has not.

## Questions you might reasonably have

**Can my head of department see my appraisal?** They can see what their scope
and permissions allow. Development supervision and pay information are
deliberately separate — supervising your development does not give access to
your increment position.

**Can another teacher see my records?** No. Access is enforced by the database
on every row, not by the screen you are looking at.

**Does the platform know my salary?** No. It holds no salary figures at all. It
records which pay arrangement applies and on whose authority, never an amount.

**Can I be marked down for an honest self-assessment?** Your self-assessment is
one input among several and is never the verified level. An honest one makes the
conversation useful; an inflated one wastes it.
